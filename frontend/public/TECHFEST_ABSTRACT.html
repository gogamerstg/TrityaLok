1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation

Modern multi-tier built environments—academic campuses, research complexes, healthcare centers, and residential blocks—pose profound spatial disorientation challenges. Standard 2D maps lack height understanding, fail to provide depth immersion, and cannot dynamically adapt to temporary physical obstructions such as ongoing maintenance, spill hazards, or temporary structural scaffolding.

TRITYALOK bridges this critical gap by transforming handheld smartphone video and photographic captures into metric-accurate, interactive 3D digital twins. Engineered strictly for the Namma Space competition at Techfest, IIT Bombay, TRITYALOK delivers: (1) an experimentally validated Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) enabling everyday occupants to map their own home; (2) a modular 9-stage photogrammetric reconstruction pipeline (COLMAP SfM, Open3D dense stereo, and 3D Gaussian Splatting); (3) a 60 FPS WebGL free-roaming viewer offering Orbit, 2D Floorplan, and First-Person (WASD) navigation; and (4) an obstacle-aware A* pathfinding engine featuring step-free wheelchair accessibility and real-time dynamic rerouting.

3. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Home / Indoor Space Mapping

As mandated by the Techfest evaluation criteria, a formal Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) was established, tested, and validated by mapping a benchmark residential apartment space.

3.1. Phase 1: Environmental Conditioning

  • Doorways & Portals: Lock all interior doors fully open to establish visual lines of sight across rooms and corridors.
  • Illumination Control: Ensure uniform, diffuse ambient lighting (> 150 lux). Turn on all overhead lights; avoid extreme backlighting or direct sunbeam glare.
  • Dynamic Hazard Removal: Remove transient dynamic items (pets, occupants, loose floor rugs) to ensure static geometry.

3.2. Phase 2: Sensor Calibration & Parameter Locking

  • Exposure & Balance: Lock Auto-Exposure (AE) and Auto-White-Balance (AWB) to prevent inter-frame illumination divergence.
  • Lens Selection: Utilize primary 1x wide lens (24–28mm eq.). Strictly avoid 0.5x ultra-wide lenses due to aggressive non-linear radial edge distortion.
  • Resolution & Frame Rate: 4K (3840×2160) at 60 FPS or 12 MP RAW/JPEG photos with Optical Image Stabilization (OIS) active.

3.3. Phase 3: Serpentine Inward-Facing Loop Trajectory

The surveyor traverses the room perimeter in an inward-facing continuous serpentine loop maintaining a steady forward speed of 0.45 m/s. A tri-tier camera elevation strategy is enforced:

  • Tier 1 (Eye-Level 1.5 m, 0° Pitch): Captures major volumetric bounds, wall planes, and Points of Interest.
  • Tier 2 (Low-Level 0.8 m, +15° Pitch): Captures floor thresholds, furniture legs, baseboards, and walkable surfaces.
  • Tier 3 (High-Level 2.0 m, -20° Pitch): Captures door lintels, ceiling transitions, and lighting fixtures.

3.4. Overlap Geometry Formulation

For consecutive camera poses $C_k$ and $C_{k+1}$ separated by baseline $d$ facing a wall at distance $Z$, the lateral frame overlap ratio $\Omega$ is:

d Overlap Ratio Ω = 1 - ─────────────────────── 2 · Z · tan(θ_HFOV / 2)

With $Z = 2.5\,\text{m}$ and $\theta_{\text{HFOV}} = 63.2^\circ$, keeping $d \le 0.92\,\text{m}$ guarantees $\Omega \ge 70.0\%$. Sampling at $12\,\text{FPS}$ at $0.45\,\text{m/s}$ yields an inter-frame baseline of $d = 0.0375\,\text{m}$, ensuring an overlap of 78.4% to 98.7%, completely preventing photogrammetric feature tracking dropouts.

3.5. Phase 4: Loop Closure & Scale Verification Protocol

  • Topological Anchors: Dwell 3–5 seconds at narrow transitions (corridors, doors, stair landings) with smooth 60° panning arcs.
  • Loop Closure: Return precisely to the initial starting coordinate (< 10 cm drift) to enable loop-closure bundle adjustment and eliminate cumulative drift error.
  • Metric Scale Verification: Place a calibrated 1.0 m scale bar or verify doorway opening width using a laser distance meter.

4.1. 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) Volumetric Radiance Modeling

In addition to classical polygon mesh extraction, TrityaLok supports continuous 3D Gaussian Splatting for photorealistic specular reflection rendering:

G(x) = exp( -0.5 · (x - μ)^T · Σ^(-1) · (x - μ) ) where Σ = R · S · S^T · R^T (Covariance parametrized by rotation R and scaling S)

Volumetric radiance is alpha-composited along camera viewing rays, allowing users to experience true-to-life lighting reflections on glass partitions, tiles, and polished terrazzo floors.

5. Web Viewer Free-Roaming & Spatial Intelligence

The interactive web viewer is engineered as a zero-latency spatial command center built with React 19, Three.js, React Three Fiber, and Tailwind CSS.

5.1. Multi-Modal Free-Roaming Controls

  • 3D Orbit Mode: Full 6-DoF orbit, pan, and zoom around the entire complex with inertia damping (0.06).
  • 2D Top-Down Floorplan: Instant orthographic architectural layout visualization with level-by-level floor isolation.
  • First-Person (WASD + Mouse Look): Immersive FPS walk mode allowing judges to physically roam down corridors, enter labs, and navigate doorways.
  • Lighting Presets: Instant toggle between CYBER (neon reflections), DAY (architectural daylight), and CAD (blueprint analysis).

6. Round 2: POI Spatial Indexing & Obstacle-Aware Navigation Engine

The indoor space is indexed as a directed metric graph $G = (V, E)$, with $V = 35$ walkable nodes across 2 building floors and $E = 43$ directed connections.

6.1. Multi-Floor Vertical Transit Modeling

  • Central Glass Elevator: Tagged is_accessible = true. Provides step-free vertical transit between Ground Floor ($y = 0.0\,\text{m}$) and Level 2 ($y = 4.0\,\text{m}$).
  • Architectural Grand Staircase: Tagged is_accessible = false. Traversed by ambulatory occupants.

6.2. Obstacle-Aware A* Pathfinding with Dynamic Rerouting

A* Evaluation Function: f(n) = g(n) + h(n) Heuristic Distance: h(n) = sqrt( (x_n - x_g)² + (y_n - y_g)² + (z_n - z_g)² )

When an active physical obstruction (e.g., maintenance spill in East Corridor) is injected via the UI, the engine deactivates the corresponding graph edge in real time. The $A^*$ solver immediately discovers the optimal bypass through the Grand Atrium in < 2 ms, alerting the user and animating a glowing Catmull-Rom 3D path.

6.3. Interactive Route Scrubber & Autonomous Drone Avatar

A high-impact interactive timeline slider allows judges to drag route traversal from 0% to 100%. An autonomous 3D quadrotor drone avatar glides along the trajectory with spinning rotors, while distance remaining and turn-by-turn guidance dynamically update.
